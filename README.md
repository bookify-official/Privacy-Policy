  <h1>Bookify privacy policy</h1>
  <p class="muted">Last updated: October 21, 2026</p>

  <p class="summary"><strong>Short version: Bookify doesn't collect any data about you.</strong> There are no Bookify servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your books never leave your device. The little Bookify does keep about you (your name, your answers to a few questions, your reading time) stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, where we can't see it.</p>

  <h2>Signing in</h2>
  <p>Bookify uses Sign in with Apple. Apple gives the app an anonymous ID for you and, the first time only, the name and email you choose to share (which can be a private relay address). Bookify keeps these in your device's Keychain and in your own iCloud account, so your other devices recognize you. They are never sent to us. Settings → Account → Delete account removes them from your devices and your iCloud; to stop Sign in with Apple for Bookify entirely, open the Settings app → your name → Sign in with Apple.</p>

  <h2>The questions when you start</h2>
  <p>When you first open Bookify it asks a few optional questions (why you read, what you read, how much, and a daily goal). Your answers are used only on your device: to set your daily goal and to start "Find free books" on textbooks for students. They are stored on your device and in your own iCloud, never sent to us.</p>

  <h2>Reading time</h2>
  <p>Bookify counts how long you listen, by day and by book, to show your goal, streak and reading time. These totals are stored on your device and in your own iCloud, so they add up across your devices. We can't see them.</p>

  <h2>Your books</h2>
  <p>Books and documents you add are processed and stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Speech is made on your device by the built-in voices; no text is sent to a server.</p>

  <h2>Photos of pages and scanned PDFs</h2>
  <p>When you add photos of pages, or a PDF that is a scan, Bookify finds the words with text recognition that runs on your device (Apple's Vision framework). The photos and the text found in them stay on your device. If you take photos with the camera or pick them from your library, Bookify only uses them to read the pages you chose.</p>

  <h2>Your settings and listening progress</h2>
  <p>Bookmarks, saved highlights, voice, speed, text size and appearance settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.</p>
  <p>Your place in each book (how far you've listened, and the book's title) is also saved to your own iCloud account, so it follows you to your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. This uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage: it is stored by Apple under your Apple ID, and we can't see it. Books themselves are never uploaded. You can turn this off in Bookify's Settings → Sync.</p>
  <p>"Find free books" searches and downloads books you choose from Project Gutenberg, OpenStax and the Internet Archive, and "Add from a link" fetches the page or file you enter (including shared Google Docs, Slides, Sheets and Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive files). Those sites see an ordinary request from your device; nothing about you is sent with it. While your device charges, Bookify may make the audio for the next part of your book in the background; this happens on your device.</p>

  <p>When you share something to Bookify from another app, it's saved on your device for Bookify to read in. It isn't sent anywhere.</p>

  <h2>Purchases and tips</h2>
  <p>Bookify Pro and tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details or your Apple ID. Apple tells the app only whether you own Pro, so it can unlock Pro features. See <a href="https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/">Apple's privacy policy</a>.</p>

  <h2>Third parties</h2>
  <p>Bookify contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it doesn't share data with anyone.</p>

  <h2>Children</h2>
  <p>Bookify doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.</p>

  <h2>Changes</h2>
  <p>If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.</p>

  <h2>Contact</h2>
  <p>Questions? Email <a href="mailto:bookify.helpdesk@gmail.com">bookify.helpdesk@gmail.com</a>.</p>
